MIBLY — TWO HTML PRESENTATIONS

Product: product.html — 7 slides, about 3:30 with the live demo.
AI process: process.html — 6 slides, about 2:30.

Click the cover to start the elapsed timer. The first click keeps you on the cover.
Advance with the arrow buttons, left/right keys or Space. Slides never auto-advance.
N opens speaker notes and sources. Escape closes notes. F toggles full screen.
Pause, Resume, Reset and Finish are available in the presentation controls.
The timer marks the assignment's target range and warns when you go over.

Phone screens are live, interactive instances of the app, not screenshots.
Feature labels sit on the left; the interactive phone sits on the right.
Click a label to open that feature. Navigation inside the phone updates the
highlighted label and the "On the phone" caption automatically.
Meals & feed includes Restaurant meal and Home cooking shortcuts.
Friends & discovery includes a Friends of friends shortcut.
Each slide keeps its own phone state until you Reset the presentation.
Actions inside these embedded phones do not change the main demo's stored state.
The timer keeps running while you interact with the phone.
For a smoother recording, try Copy address instead of switching to Google Maps.

Serve the complete package over HTTP and open /slides/product.html or
/slides/process.html. Keep the assets and app bundle with the HTML files.
The embedded app uses browser modules, so double-clicking file:// is not supported.
