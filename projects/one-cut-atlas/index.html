HOW TO PLAY

One clean hit.

Read the blade. Control the distance.

A / DMove. Arrow keys work too.
MousePoint high, mid or low over the arena to set your blade line. Left button charges and strikes, right button evades.
W / X / SHigh / mid / low stance. Or 1 / 2 / 3. Standing in the same line as a cut blocks it.
JTap to cut, hold to charge a longer lunge. Switch to a neighbouring line right after a tap to feint.
KHold a firm guard: a harder parry and a longer counter window.
LCounter after a successful guard or evade.
Space / CEvade vertical cuts / duck mid cuts.
VShove at close range.
MToggle music and effects.
Esc / RPause / retry after the result.
PhotoDrop a travel photo on the home screen. The scout finds the place, the globe unlocks it, and the duel is fought in a painted pixel arena of that place.
