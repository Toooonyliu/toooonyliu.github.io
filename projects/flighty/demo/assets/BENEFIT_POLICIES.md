# Membership benefits in this prototype

Policy review: September 16, 2026. Sample/manual accounts; no live membership verification.

The account card shows the explicitly entered current tier and validity. Proposed credit never activates a tier. Recognized manual entries follow the same rules as sample entries. Missing, expired and unsupported tiers are not assigned invented privileges.

## Policy coverage

- **ANA Bronze / Platinum:** [ANA comparison](https://www.ana.co.jp/en/jp/amc/premium/overview/service-comparison/) and [airport eligibility](https://www.ana.co.jp/en/jp/amc/premium/service/priority-flight/detail/). Bronze has Premium Economy counter access on ANA Group international flights, additional international baggage subject to allowance rules, and eligible ANA Lounge entry using miles or Upgrade Points. Lounge entry is not generally free for Bronze. Platinum previews eligible lounge and priority services.
- **United Premier Silver / Gold:** [United RewardsPlus FAQ](https://marriottbonvoy.unitedmileageplus.com/faqs) and [United PerksPlus reward chart](https://unitedperksplus.united.com/chart.aspx). Silver has Economy Plus at check-in when available and Premier Access airport services where available. Gold previews Economy Plus at booking for the member and one companion when available. These are selected benefits, not a complete membership catalog. Full [United Premier terms](https://www.united.com/en/us/fly/mileageplus/premier/benefits.html) apply.
- **BA Bronze / Silver:** [Tier benefits](https://www.britishairways.com/content/the-british-airways-club/about-the-club/tiers-and-benefits), [seat selection](https://www.britishairways.com/content/information/seating/reserving-your-seat) and [oneworld privileges](https://www.britishairways.com/content/information/partners-and-alliances/oneworld/frequent-flyer-benefits). Bronze seat selection on BA starts seven days before departure; exit-row/group exceptions apply. Silver previews seat selection at booking and eligible business lounge access.
- **Japan Airlines partner case:** BA Bronze means oneworld Ruby priority check-in on eligible JAL / American flights. Do not transfer BA's seven-day seat policy to JAL. Ruby does not itself confer lounge access; see [JAL lounge rules](https://www.jal.co.jp/jp/en/jalmile/flyon/lounge.html). BA Silver previews Sapphire lounge eligibility with conditions.

## Evaluation and assumptions

The planner evaluates the selected itinerary's assumed operating airlines and the full departure-to-return membership validity period. PIT connections are United for ANA itineraries and American for JL-marketed JAL itineraries. A United Economy Plus preview applies only to an Economy leg, including the assumed Economy connection when the long-haul cabin is Premium Economy.

The strongest matching saved membership is shown, prioritizing coverage of route legs, with stable account ordering for equal coverage. Other saved memberships may also have benefits; this bounded catalog does not model every alliance combination. Benefit sorting prioritizes this coverage then the lowest sample fare. It does not assign a cash value to perks or declare a universal best airline.

Selected benefits appear separately from illustrative fare inclusions. Bags and the Business-cabin lounge inclusion are fare-scenario assumptions, not a live ticket entitlement. A membership must be recognized on the booking; choosing a different credit program does not transfer status. Travelers must confirm dual recognition with the airline where relevant.

Account policies use the prototype's frozen scenario date; itinerary checks use the chosen travel dates. No policy auto-refresh exists. Dates, ticket conditions, lounge rules and available seating must be checked again before production or booking. Unsupported tiers remain saved and editable without fabricated benefit lists.

Base mileage calculations and their coverage are documented separately in [EARNING_RULES.md](EARNING_RULES.md).
