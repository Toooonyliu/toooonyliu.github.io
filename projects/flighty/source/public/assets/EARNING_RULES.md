# Planner earnings — September 16, 2026

This demo calculates **base flight earnings**, rather than assigning fixed earning packages. It is not a live fare quote or final airline credit. Account balances are entered/sample values. Comparing flights never changes them.

## Calculation coverage

- ANA: published Tokyo–SFO (5,130), Tokyo–ORD (6,283) sector miles; H Economy 70%, E Premium 100%, D Business 125%. Domestic United connections use H for Economy/Premium and D for Business. Award miles are rounded down per segment. Premium Points add 400 boarding points for these 100%/125% classes; North America–Japan route factor is 1. Only ANA-operated segments count toward ANA Group points.
- United: United-issued 016 standard paid tickets purchased after April 2, 2026; base general-member rate 3 miles per eligible USD, 1 PQP per eligible USD and 1 PQF per eligible segment. Premier/card bonuses are excluded. This credit choice links to United, including on the ANA itinerary. It must not be used for an ANA-issued ticket.
- JAL credited to BA: JL-marketed international long-haul H/E/D earns 70%/100%/250% base Avios; the AA-operated JL codeshare connection uses H (70%) or D (125%). The calculation uses geographic miles as an approximation to airline sector mileage. Tier-point percentages use the published JL table. No ANA credit is assigned.
- BA: base Blue rate of 6 Avios per eligible GBP, plus 1 tier point per eligible GBP and the standard long-haul cabin bonus (150/275/500). Excludes tier bonuses and add-ons.

Sample fares exclude assumed non-earning taxes of $140 per direction internationally and $25 for Chicago. Eligible spend is divided equally between outward and return directions and rounded down to whole currency units. BA uses an illustrative 0.75 GBP/USD conversion; actual tickets use the airline's purchase-date IATA rate. Reverse directions assume identical fare classes and distances. ANA's published Tokyo mileage applies only to ANA-operated sectors; other sectors use approximate geographic miles.

The UI labels base earnings, lists the per-direction formulas and assumptions, and links to the airline rules. Dates filter goal contributions; they do not query live fares. All remaining goals, counters and eligibility must be verified with the airline. No earning totals are imported or posted automatically.

## Visual meaning

Purple is current entered credit. Yellow is the proposed trip's in-period contribution. Gray is the remaining distance to the goal. If the projection exceeds the target, the scale expands to show all projected credit, with the target and resulting balance still labeled numerically. Other booked and pending credits are excluded from this comparison.
